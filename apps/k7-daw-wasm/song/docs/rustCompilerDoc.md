# The ProjectDoc in Rust (2h)

Sub-step 2h of [rustCompiler.md](rustCompiler.md), whose decisions 1 to 120, output rules, hostile-input rules and stage gate it keeps; 2e's, 2f's and 2g's
decisions are in [rustCompilerLanes.md](rustCompilerLanes.md), [rustCompilerClips.md](rustCompilerClips.md) and [rustCompilerCurves.md](rustCompilerCurves.md).
It joins what they answer into the document the engine plays: the plugins and sends of each track, the clips on its halves, the baked automations, the
frozen points and the automatic freezes, the colours and the order, the console and the recorder, and the whole `compile` with its gate. Nothing here is
wired to a route (300). Paths are relative to `apps/k7-daw-wasm/song`.

## Corrections to the 2h row of `rustCompiler.md`

- The row omits `project.ts`' `loadProjectWithFreezes` and `withGroupMix`; `ownMix.ts`; `console.ts`' `withConsole` (2e left it here, 279); `basePreset.ts`' `isSurgePatch`;
  song-schema's `trackKind.ts` (`orderTracks`, `trackCategory`) and `trackColor.ts` (`autoColors`); `moduleTypes.ts`' `ownLevelSide`, `ownMixSide`, `pluginModule`, `laneSoundRef` and the three stage param names;
  `surfaces.ts`' `projectSurfaces` (the recorder asks it); `autoFreeze.ts`' `thawedIn`, a per-process state.
- `autoFreeze.ts` reads `loadGraph`, which is 2k's: 2h reads the same facts off `Inputs` (339).
- The spec's `Request` has no way to say which modules are thawed: `Request::thawed` joins it (303).
- `compare.rs` did not hold two of the rules "equal enough" lists: a lane compared as a function and a render's name with its fingerprint masked (355).
- The gate row says every golden `engine` document: 175 of the 177 are documents and 2 are `null` (a failed compile). The golden corpus holds no freeze, so the row cannot see one; the sweep and the negative corpus do.
- 2f's decision 150 (2h derives the twins) is replaced by 301: no reader of a `ProjectDoc` twin exists.
- The 2,000 lines are low: the Rust is about 4,400 lines and its tests and scripts about 5,000.
