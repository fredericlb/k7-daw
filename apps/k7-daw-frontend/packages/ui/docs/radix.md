# Behaviour on Radix

The kit's interactive components take their behaviour from Radix primitives and keep only the look (classes, tokens, the sliding pill). Each line says what upstream owns and why we do not hand-write it.

- **`editor-dock.tsx`**: Radix Dialog like `side-panel.tsx`, non-modal on Pad and Desk. The phone `EditorSheet` is non-modal at partial height and becomes modal when expanded, so the lanes above it stay usable until it covers them. Details in `editor-dock.md`.
- **`side-panel.tsx`**: Radix Dialog. `Sheet` is modal (focus trap, focus back to the opener, scroll lock, Escape); `SidePanel` is non-modal and ignores outside interaction, since people work in the panes while it is open. Both render in place, not in a portal, because the host positions them under the header. `SidePanel` keeps `role="complementary"`; the host's `onEscapeKeyDown` decides when the app's Esc ladder, not the panel, takes the key.
- **`picker.tsx`**: Radix Popover, not Select: Select's items cannot hold a search field, and the list must keep a name plus a path per row. Popover brings collision-aware placement, Escape and outside-click dismissal and focus return; the list itself is `search-list.tsx`.
- **`point-menu.tsx`**: not Radix. A menu opened at a pointer has no trigger to anchor to, which Radix menus need, so the kit owns one contract: `useDismiss` (an outside press in the capture phase, an outside scroll, the window losing focus, Escape), `usePointClamp` (8 px inside the viewport), arrows, Home, End and Enter on the rows, 44 px touch rows, `--k7-z-menu`. The context menu, the builder grid's menu, the file tree's menu and the module/asset/preset picker share it; a menu with a trigger stays a Radix `dropdown-menu`.
- **`search-list.tsx`**: a combobox, not a Radix part. Focus stays in the field, the arrows move a highlight (`aria-activedescendant`), Enter picks it. Rows are ranked in one batched call per query through `setSearchRank`, which the app points at Rust's fuzzy scorer; `rank={null}` is for a host that already filtered. `picker.tsx`, the tree picker's search, the chain list and the icon picker draw it, and an empty list is the one `empty-row.tsx`.
- **`dialog.tsx`**: Radix Dialog. `layer="panel"` stacks it under the menus and pickers, and `nonModal` (with `modal={false}` on the root) draws the scrim itself and ignores outside presses, for a dialog whose pickers and toasts must stay reachable.
- **`choice.tsx`**: Radix RadioGroup in both layouts: `radiogroup`/`radio` roles, arrow-key selection and the one tab stop are upstream's. The sliding pill (`lib/lozenge.ts`) reads `aria-checked` and stays ours.
- **`chip.tsx`**: the filter variant is a Radix Toggle: `aria-pressed` and its `data-state` come from upstream; the other variants are plain markup.
